\section{Expected Results}


Based on previous research on Large Language Models and prompt engineering,
this study expects several possible observations.



\subsection{Potential Improvement from Structured Prompts}


Structured prompts may improve solution correctness by providing additional
guidance for algorithm selection, complexity analysis, and verification.


Compared with direct prompting, optimized prompts may help models generate more
consistent solutions.



\subsection{Potential Benefits of AI-Assisted Workflow}


A multi-step AI-assisted workflow may provide advantages on complex problems by
encouraging:

\begin{itemize}

\item Multiple solution exploration.

\item Trade-off analysis.

\item Error verification.

\item Optimization.

\end{itemize}



\subsection{Possible Limitations}


Despite potential improvements, LLM-assisted programming may still have
limitations:


\begin{itemize}

\item Incorrect reasoning.

\item Hallucinated algorithms.

\item Inefficient implementations.

\item Difficulty handling unfamiliar problem variations.

\end{itemize}



The final experimental results will determine whether these expected benefits
are supported by empirical evidence.
